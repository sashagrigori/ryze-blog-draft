    Bids, budgets, negatives and pauses are made by the agent, and approval settings decide which ones wait for you. Opteo, Adalysis and Adzooma recommend and wait for a click.

    One flat price.

    Paid Ads Autopilot is $89 a month at any spend, with a 7-day free trial. OptyAds starts at $209 and Opteo at $129, and both rise with the spend they manage.

    CaveatAgencies that want their own rule logic get more control from OptyAds.
    Reason 03

    More than Google.

    The same agent covers Meta, TikTok and LinkedIn, which matters when the budget question is between channels.

    CaveatAdalysis goes deeper on RSA testing, and Opteo on Google-only one-click fixes.

    The free Google Ads MCP connector lets Claude or ChatGPT query the account in plain language.

    CaveatRyze AI is a younger company than OptyAds or WordStream, which have a decade of track record.
